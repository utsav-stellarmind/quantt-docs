QUANTT Platform — Project Scope Document

1. Project Overview
QUANTT is an AI-powered autonomous trading platform built on the Lithosphere blockchain.
Instead of a human watching charts manually, a user deploys an AI agent that:
Researches the market
Makes a trade decision
Checks risk rules
Executes the trade on a DEX
Records every decision on-chain
All of this happens automatically, 24/7.
For companies (B2B), QUANTT also exposes the same AI engine as an API service, billed in QUANTT tokens with on-chain payment verification.
